Frequently asked questions

Can I convert HEIC or HEIF images?

Not in this initial release. HEIC/HEIF decoding needs additional browser-compatible code, testing and licensing review.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. Transparent areas will be replaced with a white background.

Why did my file get larger?

PNG can be much larger than JPG or WebP because it preserves image data losslessly.
