Frequently asked questions

Will this always produce a 50 KB file?

Not always. Results depend on source image complexity, resolution and browser encoding. The tool clearly warns you if it cannot meet the limit.

Can I keep my original image dimensions?

Yes. Uncheck the option to allow dimension reduction. This can make strict file-size limits harder to satisfy.

Does my image get uploaded?

No. This version processes the image locally in your browser using Canvas APIs.
