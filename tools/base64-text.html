Encode or decode UTF-8 text with Base64

Paste text, choose Encode to Base64 or Decode from Base64, and generate the result. This is useful when text must travel through a Base64-compatible channel.

Example and important limitation

For example, encode a short UTF-8 string while testing an application. Base64 is reversible encoding—not encryption—so never use it to conceal passwords or other secrets.
